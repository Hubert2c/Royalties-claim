      How you get paid

      1. Register each song with its ISWC, the recording's ISRC and the split between all writers.
      2. Every co-writer confirms their share. Until then, money for that song is held, not lost.
      3. Radio and TV plays are detected by audio fingerprint; concerts, adverts and venues report what they play.
      4. Each month the Government calculates a distribution run and pays you by mobile money or bank.
      5. Your statement shows every play behind every franc. If something looks wrong, raise a dispute.
